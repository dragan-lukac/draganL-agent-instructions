### Key design points worth remembering
- **Two file layers.** `song.txt` = chords above lyrics (what Dragan reads, what goes to
  Notion). `song.pro` = chord anchors bound to character offsets (internal). Anchors mean
  a capo or transpose change is a re-render, not a hand re-edit. Both parse back in.
- **Tracks have roles**, not filenames: `--guitar` drives chord recognition and tabs,
  `--vocals` drives lyrics alignment, `--mix` is playback and the fallback for anything
  missing. A single phone recording is just the case where all roles resolve to one file.
- **Recording on a DI instead of a phone is the single biggest accuracy lever** —
  ~70% → ~90%+ chord accuracy, and it removes the need for Demucs separation entirely.
  Phone mics roll off below ~150 Hz, losing the chord root, which is what causes constant
  C↔Am / G↔Em confusion.
- **Section clustering is the UX lever.** Songs repeat, so cluster repeated sections and
  confirm ~8 unique bars rather than 200 individual chord events.
- **The review loop is the product**, not the recognition. Even at 90%, voicings and
  sus/add colour need a human ear.
- Notion needs a **code block** — the only block type that preserves the monospace spacing
  that chords-above depends on.
